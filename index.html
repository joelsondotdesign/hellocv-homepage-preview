Questions

Got questions? Find answers to common questions and get the clarity you need for a hassle-free experience!

What is hello.cv, and how does the AI career platform work?

HelloCV is an AI-powered career platform that helps you create a professional, customizable online profile at your own .cv domain. This is called a digital cv, or e-resume or e-cv. Our tools make it easy to build an impressive resume, generate tailored cover letters, and discover job opportunities — all in one place. Think of it as your personal career OS, powered by AI.

Do I need any tech skills to use the AI resume and profile builder?

Not at all. Describe what you want in plain language and the builder does the rest — no code, no design tools.

How does the AI cover letter generator work?

It uses your profile and the job you're targeting to draft a tailored cover letter you can edit and export.

Can I customize my AI-generated profile and cover letters?

Yes — everything is editable. Chat to make changes, or fine-tune manually with full control.

How much does it cost to create a profile with hello.cv?

You can start for free. Paid plans add unlimited websites, your own domain, and the full AI toolkit.

Are .cv profiles mobile-friendly and responsive?

Yes — every template is responsive and looks great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Will my .cv profile show up on Google and search engines?

Yes. SEO and AI-discoverability (GEO/AEO) are built in, so you surface wherever people search.

How is hello.cv different from LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is a network you rent a page on. hello.cv is your own domain, your own design, and an AI agent that speaks for you.

Can I link my .cv profile to LinkedIn and other platforms?

Absolutely — add it to your LinkedIn, email signature, bios, and business cards. One link for everything.

How does the job matching feature work?

Your profile powers a personalized job feed — the agent surfaces roles that match your skills and preferences.
